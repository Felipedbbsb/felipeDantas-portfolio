01 / about

I design expressive
systems.

I am a Computer Science graduate from the University of Brasília (UnB), specializing in creative technology and game development. I served as principal programmer and game designer on Dream Delirio’s and XIII — A Final Game of Tarot with Death. XIII is available on Steam, while Dream Delirio’s has a Steam page and a playable demo. My work spans gameplay systems, Unity and Godot development, custom C++/SDL2 engines, shaders, procedural graphics, audio integration with FMOD and Wwise, and performance optimization. I am also a Guest Lecturer in Computer Graphics at UnB.
